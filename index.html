Our Mission

At Connect Power, our mission is to empower individuals and organizations to achieve their full potential by connecting talent with opportunity. We believe every person in Nigeria and Africa deserves meaningful work, and every employer deserves exceptional talent. We are dedicated to delivering quality connections and strive for excellence in matching talent to the right opportunities, resulting in long-lasting, fulfilling employment relationships.

Privacy Policy

Read our full Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Connect Power (CP) Recruitment Portal collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

  1. Data Collection — We collect personal data such as name, contact details, date of birth, educational background, and other information required for recruitment purposes.
  2. Purpose of Processing — Your data is processed solely for the purpose of recruitment, verification, and communication regarding your application.
  3. Data Sharing — Your information may be shared with authorized government agencies as required by law. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
  4. Data Security — We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
  5. Data Retention — Your data will be retained only as long as necessary for recruitment and legal purposes.
  6. Your Rights — You have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal requirements.
  7. Contact — For privacy-related inquiries, contact connectsupport@gmail.com.

By using this portal, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.
